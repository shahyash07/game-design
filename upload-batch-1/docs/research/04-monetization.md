# StaySmart monetization brief

Research brief, 24 October 2026. Persona: consumer-subscription monetization analyst. Research only.

## 1. Competitor pricing and paywall design (US, 2026)

Prices are US store or web list prices; most apps run regional and promo variants. Where sources conflict, the range is shown.

- **Elevate** (brain training). Free: one 3-game workout/day, game of the week, basic tracking. Pro: $9.99/mo (no trial) or $39.99/yr with 7-day trial. Lifetime pops up as a special offer at $139.99–199.99 (unverified). Soft paywall; upgrade prompt is shown before the score summary after the free workout.
- **Lumosity**. Free: 3 preselected games/day, permanently. Premium: $11.99/mo, $59.99/yr (App Store); family ~$99.95/yr for 5 (third-party); some regions show $109.99/yr. Premium = 40+ games, 5-game workouts, Insights reports, NCPT percentile tests. Soft paywall.
- **Peak**. Free: capped daily workout with ads. Pro: ~$34.99/yr (sales to ~$22), $8.99/mo, $6.99/wk. 12-month one-time gift option, no lifetime. Soft paywall; the personalised "Coach" program and stats graphs are the upsell.
- **Impulse** (fastest-growing in the category; est. $170k–900k/mo App Store revenue). Hard paywall: onboarding quiz, then a mandatory trial screen before any content. Weekly $6.99–9.99 with 3-day trial, annual $29.49 (offer) to $49.99, lifetime offered; a second, discounted annual paywall appears after the first session. Test results are hidden behind payment, which drives both conversion and 1-star reviews.
- **NeuroNation**. Web: 3-month plan $14/mo, 12-month $7/mo (~$84/yr), lifetime $499; App Store $13.99/mo, $41.99/yr. 14-day money-back guarantee. Soft paywall.
- **Brilliant**. Free: daily challenges and a metered "keys" allowance of a few lessons/day. Premium: $30/mo monthly, annual at 38% off (~$225/yr), Family $40/mo for 6 seats, 7-day trial. No business tier; older 3–50 seat group plan at ~$10–11/person/mo.
- **Duolingo**. Free with ads and hearts/energy. Super: ~$12.99/mo, ~$84–120/yr, family $120–144/yr for 6. Max: $29.99/mo, ~$168/yr. Max was 5% of paid subs at end-2024, 9% by Q3 2025; Duolingo is now moving Video Call down into Super and "experimenting with" Max. 9.2% of MAU are paid.
- **Headspace**. $12.99/mo (7-day trial), $69.99/yr (14-day trial), student $9.99/yr. Near-hard paywall (very little free content).
- **Calm**. $14.99–16.99/mo, $69.99–79.99/yr (increase reported Feb 2026), "Calm for Life" $499.99, 7-day trial. Near-hard paywall.
- **NYT Games**. $4.25–6.99/mo or $39.99–49.99/yr; constant 50%-off first-year offers (~$20–25). Free: daily Wordle, Connections, Strands, Mini, Pips. Paid: full Crossword, Spelling Bee past the first few words, 10,000+ archive, WordleBot analysis, weekly bonus puzzles. 10M+ daily players, 1M+ Games subscribers (2024). Explicitly "Wordle as top of funnel"; soft paywall that gates depth and archive, never the daily habit.
- **LinkedIn Premium**. Games (Queens, Tango, Zip, Pinpoint, Crossclimb, Mini Sudoku, Patches, Wend) are free to all members and are not a Premium feature; they exist for DAU and social comparison with connections. Premium Career $29.99–39.99/mo, Business $59.99–69.99/mo.
- **Blinkist**. Free: one preselected summary/day. Premium ~$79.99–99.99/yr, 7-day trial with card; monthly ($14.99–15.99) appears to have been withdrawn in 2026.
- **Headway**. Free: one summary/day. $12.99/mo, $29.99/quarter, $59.99–89.99/yr; 3-day trial after onboarding quiz, $29.99 first-year promos; no official lifetime (AppSumo $59 deals). Hard-ish quiz-to-paywall flow.

Pattern: brain-training incumbents cluster at $35–60/yr and $9–12/mo, free tier = 3 games/day, paywall on the results/insights screen. Newer entrants (Impulse, Headway) use hard quiz-to-trial funnels and weekly plans. Content-summary apps have collapsed to $60–90/yr with lifetime deals; do not anchor on them.

## 2. Benchmarks (RevenueCat SoSA 2026, 115k apps; Adapty SoIAS 2026, 16k apps)

- **Download-to-trial (D30):** Business 9.1%, Health & Fitness 6.9%, Education 6.5%, Gaming 4.4%. Adapty global install-to-trial 10.9%.
- **Trial-to-paid:** H&F 37.7% median (2025: 39.9%, top decile 68%); Adapty global 25.6%, H&F 35%. By trial length: 17–32 days 42.5%, 5–9 days 37.4%, under 4 days 25.5%. 55% of 3-day-trial cancellations happen on day 0; ~90% of all trial starts happen on day 0.
- **Download-to-paid (D35):** H&F 2.9%, Business 2.6%; Education among the lowest (no median published). Hard paywall 10.7% vs freemium 2.1%; D60 revenue per install $3.09 vs $0.38; yet 1-year retention of annual subs is identical (27% vs 28%). Hard paywalls carry higher refunds (5.8% vs 3.4%).
- **ARPU / realized LTV per payer, year 1:** H&F $35.64, Business $35.48, Productivity $24.95, Education $22.82; North America median $32. High price points earn $62 vs $29 mid vs $11 low. AI-feature apps earn 41% more per payer but monthly plans retain 36% worse.
- **Churn:** 35% of annual cancellations happen in month 1; ~72% of annual subs cancel within year 1 (up from 56%). H&F first-renewal retention is the lowest of any category (30%, Adapty). Trial subscribers retain 1.4–1.7x better than direct buyers; Education trial users show +50% LTV vs direct buyers.
- **Annual vs monthly mix:** Overall 42% monthly / 34% yearly. H&F is annual-heavy (67–68% of subs; 60.6% of revenue). Education median annual price $44.99; global medians $5.99/wk, $8–10/mo, $34.80/yr (Adapty: $12.99/mo, $38.42/yr).
- **What A/B tests win:** trial, plan duration and number of plans lift LTV in ~58–60% of tests; visual/copy changes 35%; price changes only 28% (but winners lift LTV ~46%). 69% of top-decile apps personalise paywalls vs 42% of the rest.

Brain-training-specific benchmarks are not published; use Education/H&F as the band.

## 3. Pro features that convert, and those that do not

Proven in these categories:
- **Removing the daily cap / full library.** Every brain-training incumbent gates on 3 games/day; Duolingo's Super lead benefit is unlimited hearts, not ad removal.
- **Detailed insights, percentiles and reports.** Lumosity Insights + NCPT percentile, Peak stats graphs, Elevate performance tracking, NYT WordleBot, Impulse test results: all sit behind the paywall, and the paywall is placed on the results screen. This is the category's strongest converter.
- **Personalised plan after an onboarding assessment.** Peak Coach, Elevate's personalised program, Impulse/Headway quiz-to-plan funnels; paywall personalisation is the top-decile habit.
- **Trials anchored on the annual plan** (self-selection, higher retention).

Weak or unproven:
- **Ad-free alone:** vendor data puts it at ~6% of upgrade motivations vs 26% for content access. Irrelevant for StaySmart (no ads).
- **Offline:** no conversion evidence anywhere; moot for a web app.
- **Family plans:** raise ARPA and retention (Duolingo, Lumosity, Brilliant), not free-to-paid conversion. Defer.
- **AI tutor as a separate top tier:** Duolingo Max at 2.3x Super reached only 5–9% of subs in two years and its hero feature is being pushed down into Super; AI apps churn 30–36% faster. AI feedback belongs inside Pro, metered, not as its own tier.

## 4. B2B angle

- **Budgets:** digital-only wellness runs $3–5 per employee per month; stipend/LSA wellness funding median ~$600/yr; L&D spend $846–1,400 per employee per year and falls with company size. Both budgets exist; the readiness/decision-quality story fits L&D ("sharper professionals") better than wellness.
- **Per-seat precedents:** Headspace for Work self-serve: $45/employee/yr (list $69.99), min 10 seats, annual, admin portal with aggregate anonymous reporting, dependents at 50+ seats; enterprise negotiated $12–36/employee/yr. Calm Business: ~$64/employee/yr self-serve for 5–300 seats; enterprise $12–25 negotiated. Brilliant: no business tier (Family 6 seats $40/mo). Elevate and Lumosity: no employer product found. Brain training B2B is white space.
- **Fitness-for-duty cognitive checks:** AlertMeter (Predictive Safety): 60–90 second pre-shift graphics test plus admin portal; quote-based, reseller frames value at "under $300 per employee per year". Druid (Impairment Science): consumer $1.99/mo or $14.99/yr; enterprise priced on users, frequency and term. Creyos: clinical, from ~$250/mo per practice. Fatigue Science Readi: $499–649/yr per user with wearable (predictive, not a test). Takeaway: safety-critical employers pay $300–650/employee/yr, but only under a safety/compliance driver that knowledge work lacks. Position StaySmart Teams as L&D/performance, not impairment screening (avoids the legal and HR exposure of "declaring someone unfit").

## 5. Recommendation

**Free** (top-of-funnel, NYT model): one rotating 3-game daily workout, streak, daily leaderboard, headline skill rating, one readiness check per week with a verdict only (no breakdown), one audio explainer per week. Never gate the daily habit or the social loop.

**Pro: $8.99/mo or $49.99/yr (~$4.17/mo), 7-day trial on annual only** (Elevate's structure). Rationale: sits inside the Education annual median ($44.99) and brain-training band ($35–60); RevenueCat shows higher price points earn 2x the LTV of mid ones; no 15–30% store fee on web, so test $59.99/yr in month 3. Pro includes: all 14 games unlimited plus archive; full readiness ritual with baseline, history and trend; detailed skill reports with percentile vs other AI-heavy professionals; AI reasoning feedback metered at ~30 reviews/month (controls LLM cost and the AI-churn pattern); full audio library.

**Teams: $4 per seat per month, billed annually ($48/seat/yr), minimum 5 seats, self-serve to 250 seats, quote above.** Anchored just above Headspace small-business ($45) and well above digital-wellness PEPM ($3–5). Adds an admin view with aggregate, anonymised readiness and skill trends (never individual verdicts), team leaderboard and challenges, SSO at 50+ seats, invoice billing. Sell to L&D and team leads of AI-heavy functions; skip HR wellness RFPs initially.

**Single most important paywall placement:** the results screen of the first readiness check, on day 0. Show the verdict free, blur the baseline comparison and skill breakdown, and present the annual-with-trial offer there. This is where every incumbent gates (results/insights), ~90% of trials start on day 0, and it ties the paid value to the moment of highest personal stakes. Keep it soft (a visible "continue free" link) to protect the leaderboard and sharing loop; measure, and only consider a hard quiz-to-trial funnel for paid-acquisition traffic.

**Pro hero: the readiness ritual.** It is the only addition that is (a) differentiated (no competitor has a pre-decision check), (b) recurring and personal (baseline comparison is the proven "insights" converter), (c) near-zero marginal cost, and (d) the natural Teams hook. AI reasoning feedback is the second Pro pillar, inside Pro and metered; the Duolingo Max lesson says do not make it a tier. Audio explainers are retention content, sampled free; the summary-app category shows users will not pay much for them on their own.

Caveats: prices are US list and conflict across sources for Calm, NYT and Blinkist; Education-specific conversion medians are not public; no brain-training-category benchmark exists, so Education/H&F bands are proxies.
